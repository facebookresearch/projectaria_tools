Solutions to issues you may encounter while recording, streaming, or calibrating with the Aria Companion App.

To report a problem to the Aria team from inside the app, see [Companion App Rage Shake](/ark/support/rageshake).

## Streaming

### Streaming Not Allowed

> Streaming is not allowed with these glasses.

The glasses are not provisioned for streaming.

**Solution:** Contact [AriaOps@meta.com](mailto:AriaOps@meta.com) with your device serial number.

### Streaming via Wi-Fi is incompatible with Wi-Fi scanning

> Streaming via Wi-Fi is incompatible with Wi-Fi scanning.
>
> Please choose a recording profile without Wi-Fi scanning.

A profile the session is using enables Wi-Fi scanning, which cannot run at the same time as Wi-Fi streaming.

The message says "recording profile" because the app uses one profile format throughout, but the profile to change depends on the session type: for a **Streaming** session it is the **Streaming Profile**, and for a **Recording & Streaming** session check the **Recording Profile** as well.

**Solution:** Select a profile that does not enable Wi-Fi scanning. See [Recording Profiles](/ark/companion-app/recording-profiles).

### Unable to connect to Wi-Fi

> Unable to connect to Wi-Fi. Please check your Wi-Fi settings and try again.

The glasses could not join the network.

**Solution:** Open the glasses details screen from the dashboard and re-check the Wi-Fi setup. Confirm the network is reachable and that the glasses are in range.

### A session will not stop

> Streaming Stop Error — Press the glasses' Capture button to stop.

Or, when the request timed out:

> Streaming Stop Timed Out — Glasses may still stop streaming after an additional delay. If not, press the glasses' Capture button to stop.

The app could not confirm that the glasses stopped the session.

**Solution:** Press the Capture button on the glasses to stop the session directly. If that does not work, use **Report** in the alert to send a report.

## Eye Tracking Calibration

### Calibration Unsuccessful

> We were unable to process your eye gaze data.

The calibration ran but the gaze data could not be processed.

**Solution:** Check that the glasses are well seated on the face, that no face coverings are in the way, and that the lighting is even and moderate — avoid facing a window, a bright light, or a reflective surface. Then run the calibration again. See [Eye Tracking Calibration](/ark/companion-app/start#eye-tracking-calibration) for the full requirements.

### Face Not Detected

> If the circle is empty, the phone did not detect a face. Please check phone camera and lighting.

The phone's face tracking cannot see your face.

**Solution:** Hold the phone vertically at arm's length, as you would to take a selfie, and make sure your face is evenly lit and unobstructed.
